02 · Services

One studio. Three connected pillars.

Most teams stop at building the site or running the ads. We close the loop with the data layer underneath — so growth is visible, measurable, and repeatable.

01 — Build

Shopify stores & websites that convert

Storefront, theme, product pages, checkout flows, and the marketing apps your stack needs. Built fast, built clean, handed off documented.

  • Shopify store setup & migration
  • Custom theme & landing pages
  • Marketing site / portfolio
  • App stack: Klaviyo, reviews, upsell
02 — Grow

Campaigns, ads & lifecycle marketing

Performance campaigns and email flows run by an operator, not a dashboard. Tied to the data so we know what's actually working.

  • Meta & Google Ads management
  • Email & SMS flows (Klaviyo)
  • SEO & content foundation
  • Creative testing & iteration
03 — Measure

Analytics, pipelines & reporting

The piece most studios skip. We connect Shopify, ad platforms and tools into one warehouse, with dashboards built for founders and marketers.

  • Data pipelines (Airbyte, dbt)
  • BigQuery warehouse setup
  • Looker / Looker Studio dashboards
  • Custom scraping & integrations
Operate — monthly retainer
Build + Grow + Measure as an ongoing partnership. We run the store day-to-day so you can run the business.

07 · FAQ

Common questions.

If yours isn't here, just ask in the booking form — we reply personally.

Do we have to use all three services?+

No. You can engage us for a single pillar — say, just a Shopify build or a reporting setup. Most clients eventually layer more on, but it's never the entry requirement.

What size of business do you work with?+

Small and mid-sized ecommerce brands and SMBs — usually founder-led teams that need senior help without an agency-sized budget.

How fast can you start?+

Discovery calls within the week. Project kickoff is typically 1–2 weeks after scope is agreed. Retainers begin on the 1st of the following month.

Do you take on data-only or scraping projects?+

Yes. ELT pipelines, BigQuery setup, dbt modelling and custom scraping projects are offered standalone under the Measure pillar.
